# Settlement Failure Procedures
## COUNTERPARTY_SSI_MISMATCH
Occurs when the active account standing settlement instruction differs from the counterparty instruction on the settlement.
### Investigation
1. Retrieve the trade and settlement.
2. Confirm the account.
3. Retrieve the active SSI.
4. Compare depository and participant identifiers.
5. Confirm effective dates and status.
### Resolution
Operations must verify the correct instruction. Any change requires approval before resubmission.
